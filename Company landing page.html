What we work on

Food production machinery

Machines that run every shift need to be reliable and hygienic. We repair and maintain them so downtime stays short.

  • Packaging, filling and sealing lines
  • Mixers, slicers, conveyors and ovens
  • Preventive maintenance schedules
  • Hygienic repairs using food-safe parts and materials

CNC repair

When a CNC machine loses accuracy or stops, we find the cause and fix it, whether it is mechanical, electrical or in the control.

  • Spindle, axis and ball screw repair
  • Drives, motors and control faults
  • Alignment, calibration and accuracy checks
  • Retrofits and part replacement
